2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Registration data: name, email address, password (stored as a salted bcrypt hash) and workspace or organization name.
  • Business profile data: business name, business display name, WhatsApp Business Account identifiers and connected business phone numbers.
  • Contact directory data: names, phone numbers, WhatsApp profile names, email addresses and contact tags.
  • Content you create: message templates, chatbot rules, auto-replies, WhatsApp Flow forms, campaign settings and uploaded media.
  • Support communications: information you provide when contacting our support team.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device data: pages visited, features used, browser type, device type and approximate timestamps.
  • Authentication data: access and refresh tokens used to maintain your login session.
  • Service logs: technical records needed to operate, troubleshoot and secure the Service.

We do not use third-party advertising trackers or advertising cookies.

2.3 WhatsApp and messaging information

When a business connects its WhatsApp Business Account to UnifyLead, we may process the following on its behalf:

  • Phone numbers and WhatsApp profile names of individuals communicating with the business.
  • Inbound and outbound message content, captions, attachments and media identifiers.
  • WhatsApp Flow responses and submitted form information.
  • Conversation records, timestamps and assignment status.
  • Campaign and template delivery information, including sent, delivered, read and failed status.
  • Webhook event payloads received from Meta for messaging, account status and operational purposes.

2.4 Google Business Profile information

If a business chooses to connect its Google Business Profile account to UnifyLead, we may access and process information authorized by that business through Google's authorization process.

Depending on the permissions granted and features enabled, this may include:

  • Google account identifiers and authorized account information needed to establish the connection.
  • Business Profile account and location identifiers.
  • Business names, addresses, phone numbers, website details, operating hours and other authorized location information.
  • Customer reviews, review ratings, review text and review-related information.
  • Business Profile posts and other listing information made available through the authorized APIs.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to maintain the authorized connection.

We use this information to provide connected Business Profile features, such as displaying authorized business information, helping businesses manage their profiles and enabling review-management functionality where permitted.

We access Google information only after an authorized user connects their Google account and grants the relevant permissions. We do not request Google account passwords.

2.5 Other optional integrations

Depending on the integrations enabled by a business:

  • Shopify: we may receive order, checkout and customer webhook data, including customer names and phone numbers, and an access token used to enable configured notifications.
  • Instagram: we may receive sender identifiers and direct-message content so businesses can manage conversations through the shared inbox.
  • Meta / WhatsApp: we may receive business verification details, phone-number registration status, template approval status and messaging delivery callbacks.

  • To provide Google Business Profile features that are enabled and authorized by the connected business.

  • To help businesses prepare responses to customer reviews where permitted.

  • To communicate service updates, security notices and support information.

AI-assisted features

Where AI features are enabled, content submitted by a business or its customers may be processed by an AI service provider to generate suggested responses, classify messages or assist with configured customer-service workflows.

AI-generated content is provided as assistance and may require review and approval by the business before being sent or published.

We do not use Google Business Profile data to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

We will disclose relevant AI processing and obtain any required authorization before sending connected-service data to an AI provider.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use business customers' contact lists for our own advertising.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

  • Account information: retained while the account remains active and as necessary for legal or operational purposes.
  • Contacts and conversations: retained according to the business customer's configuration and deletion instructions.
  • Webhook event payloads: retained as necessary for operational reliability, auditing and troubleshooting.
  • Authentication credentials: retained while the relevant connection remains active and deleted or revoked when disconnected, subject to necessary security records.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information, including:

  • Passwords hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text.
  • Encryption of Meta and Shopify access tokens at rest using AES-256-GCM.
  • HTTPS/TLS for data transmitted between systems.
  • Webhook authenticity verification before processing.
  • Tenant-level access controls to help prevent one customer's information from being accessed by another customer.
  • Security headers and API access restrictions.
  • Appropriate safeguards for connected-account credentials and authorization tokens.

We do not request or store Google account passwords. Google authorization is performed through Google's OAuth authorization process.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach, we will notify affected parties and relevant authorities where required by applicable law.
