2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly (account and business data)

  • Registration data: full name, email address, password (stored only as a salted bcrypt hash), and workspace/organization name.
  • Business profile data: business name, verified business display name, WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) identifiers, and business phone numbers connected through Meta Embedded Signup.
  • Contact directory data: names, phone numbers, WhatsApp profile names, email addresses, and tags you add to your contacts.
  • Content you create: message templates, chatbot rules and auto-replies, WhatsApp Flow forms, campaign settings, and uploaded media (images, videos, documents) that you send through the Service.
  • Support communications: any information you send us when you contact support.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device data: pages visited, features used, browser type, device type, and approximate timestamps of your actions within the dashboard.
  • Authentication tokens: we issue JSON Web Tokens (access and refresh tokens) that are stored in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in.
  • Service logs: server-side records required to operate, debug, and secure the Service.

We do not use third-party advertising trackers, and we do not use cookies for advertising purposes.

2.3 Information processed on behalf of businesses (end-customer data)

When a business connects its WhatsApp number to UnifyLead, we process the following on the business's behalf:

  • Phone number and WhatsApp profile name of the person messaging the business.
  • Message content: text, captions, attachments, media identifiers, and delivery status of inbound and outbound messages.
  • WhatsApp Flow responses: answers submitted by individuals in WhatsApp Flow forms, together with the phone number they used.
  • Conversation records: conversation state, timestamps, and assignment status in the shared inbox.
  • Campaign and template delivery data: message template identifiers, language, category, and per-recipient sent/delivered/read/failed status.
  • Raw event payloads: webhook payloads received from Meta (and, if connected, from Shopify or Instagram) stored for reliability, auditing, and troubleshooting.

2.4 Information from connected integrations (optional)

  • Shopify: if you connect your store, we receive order, checkout, and customer webhook data — including customer names and phone numbers — and an access token used solely to enable WhatsApp notifications. We request read-only scopes (orders, customers, checkouts, fulfillments).
  • Instagram: if you connect Instagram Direct, we receive the sender identifier and message text of direct messages so you can reply from the shared inbox.
  • Meta / WhatsApp: business verification details, phone number registration status, template approval status, and message delivery callbacks provided through the Meta Graph API.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data is kept for as long as your account is active.
  • Contacts, conversations, and messages are retained until the business deletes them. Deleting a contact permanently removes its linked conversations and messages.
  • Webhook event payloads are retained for operational reliability, auditing, and troubleshooting.
  • Security and credential records (encrypted API tokens, authentication records) are retained while the connection is active and deleted or rotated when it is disconnected.
  • After account closure, we delete or de-identify customer content within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

Businesses can request export or deletion of their workspace data at any time by contacting immerzo2025@gmail.com.

7. Data Security

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a per-user salt and are never stored in plain text.
  • Meta and Shopify access tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM with a dedicated encryption key.
  • All traffic is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Inbound webhooks from Meta and Shopify are verified using HMAC signature validation before processing.
  • Application access is scoped per tenant, so one customer's data is never visible to another.
  • Security headers and a strict CORS origin allow-list are enforced on the API.
  • Media uploads are held in memory only for transmission and are not stored on our servers.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.
