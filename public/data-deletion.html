Data Deletion Instructions

UnifyLead — WhatsApp Business API Platform

Operated by Immerzo · Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary: You can delete your UnifyLead workspace data at any time. Send a deletion request to immerzo2025@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request", or ask us from inside the dashboard. We confirm every request and complete deletion within 30 days.

This page explains how to request deletion of the personal data that Immerzo ("UnifyLead", "we", "us", or "our") holds, and what happens to your data after a request. It supplements our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

1. Who this applies to

  • Business account owners and users — people with a UnifyLead workspace account. You can request deletion of your account and everything connected to it.
  • End customers — people who message a business through WhatsApp. The business you messaged is the controller of that data. Please contact that business first. If you cannot reach them, contact us and we will forward your request to the business and assist them in responding.

2. How to request deletion

Option A — Email (works for everyone)

  1. Email immerzo2025@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include the email address registered to your workspace, and your workspace/organization name if you know it. If you are an end customer, include the phone number you used on WhatsApp and the name of the business you messaged.
  3. We reply to acknowledge the request. We may ask you to verify your identity before deleting data, to prevent someone else from deleting your account.

Option B — From inside the dashboard

  1. Sign in to UnifyLead at the dashboard URL you normally use.
  2. You can delete individual contacts at any time from Contacts. Deleting a contact permanently removes its linked conversations and messages.
  3. To delete your entire workspace account, contact us using Option A. We will confirm before proceeding.

Option C — Facebook / Meta users

If you signed up or connected your account through Facebook or Meta Embedded Signup and want the data associated with that connection removed, use Option A. We also remove stored Meta and WhatsApp access tokens for the connection as part of the deletion. You can additionally review and remove the app from your Facebook account in Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.

3. What gets deleted

DataWhat happens on a deletion request
Account and user recordsDeleted, including name, email, and hashed password.
WhatsApp connections (WABA and phone number records)Deleted, including stored encrypted access tokens. The connection is no longer usable.
ContactsDeleted, along with names, phone numbers, and tags.
Conversations and messagesDeleted, including inbound and outbound message content and delivery status.
Templates, campaigns, bot rules, flows, and AI agent settingsDeleted, including knowledge entries, offers, and AI conversation memory.
Uploaded mediaMedia is held in memory only for transmission and is not stored on our servers, so there is nothing additional to delete.
Integration records (Shopify, Instagram)Deleted, including stored access tokens and synced identifiers.
Webhook event recordsDeleted. These payloads exist for reliability and troubleshooting only.
Audit and security logsDeleted, except where we must keep a minimal record to comply with a legal obligation or resolve a dispute.

We do not sell personal data, so there are no third-party buyer records to remove. Some data may persist in encrypted backups for a short period until the backup rotation completes, after which it is deleted.

4. How long it takes

  • We acknowledge every deletion request, normally within 7 business days.
  • We complete deletion, and confirm back to you in writing, within 30 days of verifying your request.
  • If a legal obligation requires us to retain a specific record, we will tell you what is retained and for how long.

5. Data held by Meta and WhatsApp

Messages are delivered through the WhatsApp Cloud API, operated by Meta. Deleting your UnifyLead data does not delete records Meta may hold as an independent controller. To manage that data, review WhatsApp's Privacy Policy and your WhatsApp account settings.

6. Opting out instead of deleting

If you are receiving messages from a business and only want them to stop, you do not need to delete your account. Reply STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, OPT OUT, or OPT-OUT to any message, or block or report the business inside WhatsApp. See Section 8, "Your Rights and Choices", in our Privacy Policy for details.
